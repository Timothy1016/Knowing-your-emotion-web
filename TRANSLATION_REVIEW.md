# Translation review

The automated checks verify that all 68 emotions have a non-empty name and explanation in Indonesian, English, and Mandarin, and that every “See Also” reference resolves to an existing emotion.

Culture-specific terms such as *Żal*, *Saudade*, *Awumbuk*, *Mono no aware*, *Fago*, *Litost*, *Dépaysement*, and *L’Appel du Vide* intentionally retain their original form. Mandarin display names add a short explanation while preserving the original term in parentheses where useful.

Automated consistency checks cannot certify psychological nuance or cultural accuracy. Before a public launch, the English and Mandarin text should receive a final review by native speakers familiar with mental-health and cultural terminology.
